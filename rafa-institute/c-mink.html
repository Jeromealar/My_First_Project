C MINK & Analytical Programmes

The Critical Minerals Intelligence and Knowledge Platform for Africa (C MINK) serves as RAFA's foundational proof of concept. This page provides a transparent account of its current developmental stage, provisional outputs, and upcoming thematic programmes.

Programme Reference: RAFA-PRG-CMINK-2026 • Page Last Reviewed: 18 October 2026
Phase: Prototype Validation & Scoping Version 0.4.1 (Closed Technical Preview)

Institutional Status Statement: C MINK is an Active Proof of Concept

In accordance with RAFA's institutional commitment to strict transparency, we state unequivocally that C MINK is currently in early-stage pilot validation. It is not complete, publicly operational, or fully capitalized.

C MINK was conceived to test a critical hypothesis: Can an independent, Africa-centred data platform systematically reduce information asymmetry in sovereign critical mineral negotiations?

The project is currently undergoing closed-door data stress-testing with technical teams from state geological agencies, regional economic communities, and academic mineral economists. It does not claim full continental coverage or final commercial endorsement at this stage.

Institutional Roadmap

Future Analytical Programmes in Development

Building upon the C MINK proof of concept, RAFA is structuring three additional strategic intelligence initiatives.

Pipeline 01

Sovereign Carbon & Natural Capital Accounting

Establishing transparent African methodologies for measuring, pricing, and negotiating sovereign carbon assets—including peatlands, rainforest basins, and mangrove blue carbon—to prevent predatory concession terms.

Status: Concept Note Phase
Pipeline 02

Green Hydrogen & Energy Corridor Readiness

Evaluating power-grid absorptive capacity, water desalinization requirements, and export tariff realities for large-scale green hydrogen and ammonia projects planned across North, West, and Southern Africa.

Status: Scoping Expert Working Group
Pipeline 03

AfCFTA Regional Mineral Corridor Architecture

Harmonizing cross-border rules of origin and multi-country infrastructure pooling agreements to support shared regional precursor chemical refineries and battery manufacturing clusters.

Status: Regional Dialogues Initiated
