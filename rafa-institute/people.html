Fiduciary Integrity

Funding Sources & Independence Charter

Analytical independence requires rigorous source-of-funds vetting. RAFA operates under five financial safeguards:

1. Zero Commercial Retainers

We accept no funding from commercial mining operators, equipment vendors, or private equity firms seeking concession advocacy.

2. Diversified Support

No single funding entity may account for more than 25% of RAFA's annual operational budget, preventing financial capture.

3. Editorial Firewall

All grant agreements explicitly prohibit funder review or veto over analytical findings, methodologies, or published conclusions.

4. Public Registry of Grants

All institutional philanthropic grants above $25,000 are recorded in RAFA's annual public transparency disclosure.

Methodological Rigor

Analytical Methods & Data Standards

Strategic decisions must rest on verified facts. RAFA applies a triple-verification protocol to all quantitative datasets.

1. Primary Regulatory Triangulation

Reconciling national geological registry records, audited state customs declarations, and central bank foreign exchange data to identify discrepancies in export volume reporting.

2. Commercial Contract Modeling

Benchmarking effective tax and royalty take against peer jurisdictions globally using dynamic discounted cash-flow (DCF) models calibrated for commodity price volatility.

3. Independent Expert Peer Review

Submitting all methodology notes and readiness diagnostic frameworks to external African university peer review before operational deployment with sovereign clients.
