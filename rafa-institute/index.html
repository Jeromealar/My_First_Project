Independent strategic intelligence for sovereign resource decisions.

RAFA is an Africa-centred strategic-intelligence institution. We equip African governments, regional economic communities, and multilateral bodies to navigate high-stakes mineral and energy transitions with rigorous, uncompromised evidence.

In an era of accelerating global commodity competition, decisions taken without deep analytical foresight risk locking African states into raw resource export dependencies for another generation. RAFA works to close this gap.

C MINK: Critical Minerals Intelligence and Knowledge Platform for Africa

View C MINK Status & Roadmap arrow_forward

C MINK is RAFA's foundational proof of concept. It addresses the systemic deficit of independent, cross-border intelligence on Africa's transition minerals—including lithium, cobalt, graphite, manganese, nickel, and copper. By integrating verified geological data, asset ownership tracking, and fiscal benchmark analytics, C MINK tests whether unified African data can alter sovereign negotiation outcomes.

explore Current Scope

Initial taxonomy mapping and data integration across pilot Southern and Central African mineral corridors.

Closed-door technical stress-testing with select state geological agencies and regional economic bodies.

Institutional Privacy Policy & Sovereign Data Notice

Commitment to Sovereign Data Privacy

Resource Analytics for Future Africa (RAFA) respects the confidentiality of public institutions, researchers, and strategic partners. This site collects only information volunteered directly through our briefing request and digest subscription mechanisms.

Data Protection Principles

  • No Commercial Tracking: We do not deploy third-party advertising trackers or sell institutional visitor metadata.
  • Data Minimization: We only collect information strictly required to evaluate and respond to institutional inquiries.
  • Confidentiality Safeguards: Inquiries submitted by government officials are restricted to vetted secretariat liaison officers.

Sensitive Inquiries

Do not transmit classified state secrets, proprietary concession negotiation drafts, or sensitive legal files via this public web portal. Secure cryptographic channels are coordinated directly with verified institutional counterparts.

Policy Reference: RAFA-POL-PRIV-2026-v1.2 • Approved by Secretariat Oversight.

Institutional Accessibility Statement (WCAG 2.2 AA)

Universal Access Standard

RAFA is committed to ensuring our strategic intelligence is accessible to all public officials, researchers, and citizens across Africa and globally, including those utilizing assistive technologies or operating under low-bandwidth network constraints.

Technical Compliance

  • WCAG 2.2 Level AA: High text contrast ratios (≥ 4.5:1 for body copy), scalable typography, and structured landmark regions.
  • Keyboard Navigation: All interactive elements, modal dialogs, and navigation drawers support full keyboard operability with visible focus indicators.
  • Accessible Publications: Every policy brief is provided both as a semantic HTML reading view and as an accessible PDF designed to PDF/UA standards.
  • Low-Bandwidth Optimization: Minimal external scripts, zero heavy commercial trackers, and lightweight inline SVG diagrams ensure rapid page load on 3G and rural connections.
