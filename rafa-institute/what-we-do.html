What We Do

RAFA translates complex global resource, regulatory, and financial shifts into precise decision intelligence. Five structured offerings designed for high-consequence choices.

Practice Areas: Sovereign Intelligence & Analytics • Page Last Reviewed: 18 October 2026
Analytical Offerings

Five instruments for sovereign agency.

Each offering operates under strict institutional standards, pairing quantitative market modeling with on-the-ground African institutional realities.

Offering 01

Decision Briefs

Format: Rapid-response & thematic executive briefs (4–12 pages)

Dense, highly synthesized strategic briefs focused on specific inflection points: unexpected trade tariff adjustments, new critical raw material regulations in export markets, shifts in battery chemistries, or sovereign carbon credit pricing dynamics. Written exclusively for senior decision-makers who need unclouded signal over noise.

One-line outcome: Senior decision-makers gain immediate clarity on critical trade-offs before high-stakes commitments.
Offering 02

Readiness Diagnostics

Format: Comprehensive institutional audits (3–6 month engagements)

Rigorous diagnostic assessments of national administrative architectures, state-owned enterprise absorptive capacities, statutory coherence, and cross-ministerial execution readiness. We stress-test whether announced industrial beneficiation plans can withstand infrastructure, energy, and fiscal constraints.

One-line outcome: Pinpoints critical implementation gaps and institutional vulnerabilities before capital is allocated or policies enacted.
Offering 03

Foresight

Format: Multi-horizon scenario modeling & early signal tracking

Forward-looking scenario planning that maps 5-, 10-, and 20-year transition trajectories. We model technological disruptions (such as sodium-ion battery commercialization or direct lithium extraction efficiency), shifting geoeconomic alliances, and evolving multilateral compliance frameworks to prevent sovereign stranded assets.

One-line outcome: Transitions institutions from crisis reaction to anticipatory agenda-setting.
Offering 04

Negotiation Support

Format: Confidential backstopping & fiscal term benchmarking

Confidential analytical backstopping for inter-ministerial teams negotiating major mineral development agreements, energy transition partnerships, or sovereign off-take pacts. We provide independent financial modeling, global contract benchmark comparators, and transfer-pricing stress-testing under strict non-disclosure.

One-line outcome: Balances information asymmetries so sovereign entities retain maximum local value in bilateral negotiations.
Offering 05

Convening

Format: High-level sovereign roundtables & peer-learning exchanges

Closed-door, Chatham House-rule roundtables uniting African ministers, chief negotiators, regional economic community leads, and key technical advisors. We create trusted spaces where sovereign peers can share contract intelligence, coordinate joint cross-border refining infrastructure, and align continental trade positions.

One-line outcome: Establishes unified African consensus and coordinated regional posture on consequential transition files.
